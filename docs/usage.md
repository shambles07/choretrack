# 🧹 Using ChoreTrack

[← Back to README](../README.md)

## 👥 Accounts

| | Parent | Chore doer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signs in with | Username and password | Taps their avatar, enters a 4-digit PIN |
| Can | Manage chores, people, categories and rewards; approve chores and reward requests | See and complete their own chores; spend points |

The first visit creates the first parent account. Click your avatar or name in the sidebar (top right on phones) to change your name, avatar and favourite colour. Your favourite colour also becomes the accent colour for buttons and highlights.

## ✅ Chores and approval

1. A parent creates a chore: one-off, every day, or chosen weekdays, with points, a category and an optional "done by" time.
2. The kid taps **I did it!** Confetti and a jingle play, and the chore waits for approval.
3. A parent **approves** it or **sends it back** with a note. Both can be undone.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Points, levels, streaks and trophies only change once a parent approves. Until then the points show as "Waiting".

## 🏆 Points, levels and trophies

| | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| ⭐ Points | Earned on approval. Spent in the reward shop |
| 📈 Levels | Based on lifetime points: Rookie Helper at 0, up to Legend of the House at 1,400 |
| 🔥 Streaks | Days in a row where every chore was approved. Days still waiting on approval don't break a streak |
| 🏅 Trophies | 12 to collect, from First Step to Unstoppable (a 30-day streak) |
| 🎁 Rewards | Kids request them with points. A parent approves or declines, and declined points are returned |

> [!TIP]
> Deleting a chore keeps any points already earned for it. To pause a chore instead, switch it off.
